Privacy Policy

Voice Silence Remover · Android application

Package: com.VOICESILENCEREMOVER.ai Effective date: 18 November 2026 Publisher: Voice Silence Remover
At a glance
Voice Silence Remover processes the audio you select on your device. The app does not upload your recordings to our servers, does not require an account, and does not request microphone access. The app displays ads using Unity Ads. Unity's advertising SDK collects and shares certain device and advertising-related information, as explained below. Local audio processing does not mean that the advertising SDK collects no data.

1. Who we are

Voice Silence Remover ("the app", "we", "us") is an Android application for removing silent gaps, previewing audio, applying available voice effects, and exporting processed audio. The app is published as Voice Silence Remover.

This policy applies to the Android app identified by package name com.VOICESILENCEREMOVER.ai. It explains how the app handles information and how the third-party advertising SDK handles its own data.

For privacy questions, contact shahbajbusiness@gmail.com.

2. Audio files and on-device processing

When you select an audio file, Android's system file picker gives the app access to that file. The app reads and processes the selected audio locally on your device. Silence detection, editing, voice effects, preview playback, and export are performed on the device.

  • We do not upload your selected recordings, original audio, preview audio, or exported audio to a server operated by us.
  • The original recording is not intentionally overwritten. The app creates a separate processed output.
  • Temporary preview and encoding files may be created in the app's private cache while you use the app. The app removes its managed temporary audio files during normal cleanup. Android may also clear app cache data.
  • Exports are saved to your device in Music/Voice Silence Remover. These are public media files on your device, not app-private cache files.
  • The app does not record audio from the microphone and does not request the microphone permission.

3. Information handled by the app

The app does not require registration and does not operate its own account or analytics server. It stores app preferences locally, such as appearance, silence-processing settings, voice-effect settings, and whether an introductory card has been dismissed. These preferences remain in the app's private storage.

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, or recordings to provide the audio-editing features. The Android document picker is used so you choose which audio file the app can access.

6. Permissions

  • Internet: used by Unity Ads to request and display advertisements.
  • Network state: used to check network connectivity for ad-related operation.
  • Advertising ID: declared for the advertising SDK's supported advertising functions.
  • Legacy external-storage write permission on Android 9 and older: used only to save exported audio to the public Music folder. On Android 10 and newer, exports use Android's MediaStore without broad storage permission.

The app does not request microphone, contacts, camera, SMS, precise-location, or all-files access permissions.

7. Sharing of information

We do not sell audio files or app preferences, and we do not send your selected recordings to advertising providers. However, Unity's SDK collects and shares the advertising-related categories described in Section 4. Unity may disclose or make data available to its service providers, advertising partners, and other recipients as described in Unity's privacy notices. We may disclose information when required by law or to respond to valid legal process.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • App preferences and private temporary files: stored in app-private storage. Clearing the app's data removes those preferences and private files. Uninstalling the app removes its private app data and cache.
  • Exported audio: files saved in the public Music/Voice Silence Remover folder are not removed when app data is cleared or when the app is uninstalled. You must delete those exported files yourself using a file manager or media app.
  • Unity Ads data: we do not control or directly delete data held by Unity. Uninstalling does not erase previously collected Unity data. Use the Unity privacy options or contact Unity using the details in Section 5.

We do not keep app account data or user recordings on our own servers.

9. Children's privacy

The app is not designed for or directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account system because the app has no account or registration feature. The advertising SDK may still process the categories described in Section 4 according to the age, audience, and privacy settings configured for the app. The app's actual Play Console target-audience selection and ad configuration must remain consistent with this statement and applicable Google Play Families requirements.

10. Security

Audio processing takes place on your device and the app does not transmit selected audio to our servers. The app disables cleartext HTTP traffic. Unity states that data collected by its SDK is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely risk-free, and we cannot guarantee absolute security for data handled by third parties.
