Privacy Policy

Voice Silence Remover (package name: com.VOICESILENCEREMOVER.ai)

Effective date: 6 November 2026

In short: Your audio files are processed only on your phone and are never uploaded to us. We do not ask you to create an account. The app is free and shows ads from a third-party ad network (Unity Ads), which may collect some device information as described below.

1. Who we are

Voice Silence Remover (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is an Android app that removes silent gaps from voice recordings and lets you change the sound of a voice. The app is published by Voice Silence Remover.

Questions about this policy can be sent to shahbajbusiness@gmail.com.

2. Your audio stays on your device

When you choose an audio file, the app reads it through Android’s file picker and processes it entirely on your phone: detecting silence, applying voice effects, previewing and exporting. We do not upload, store on a server, listen to, or share your audio files or the results.

  • Your original file is never changed or overwritten.
  • Files you export are saved on your device in Music / Voice Silence Remover. You can delete them at any time with any file manager or music app.
  • While you work, the app keeps temporary preview files in its private cache so you can play the result. They stay on your device and are removed when Android clears the app cache or you uninstall the app.
  • The app does not use your microphone and does not request the microphone permission.

3. Information we collect

We do not run our own servers for this app and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. There is no sign-up or login.

The app saves a few simple settings only on your device (for example: light or dark appearance, your silence and voice settings, and whether you closed the welcome card). This information never leaves your phone.

4. Advertising (Unity Ads)

The app is free and supported by advertising. It uses the Unity Ads SDK, provided by Unity Technologies, to show banner and full-screen ads. To deliver and measure ads, keep them secure, and understand app performance, the SDK needs an internet connection and may collect and use information from your device. By default, the app does not give Unity consent for personalized advertising, so ads are intended to be non-personalized.

Your ad choices

  • On Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on your phone).
  • You can stop all ad data collection by uninstalling the app.

5. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet and network state – only to load ads and check whether a connection is available.
  • Advertising ID – used by the ad SDK as described in section 4.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only) – only to save your exported audio to the Music folder. On Android 10 and newer the app uses the system media store and needs no storage permission.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. The only third party that receives data through the app is the ad provider described in section 4. We may also disclose information if required by law.

7. Data retention and deletion

We do not hold your data on our own servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. Data on your device (settings, cache, exported files) is removed when you clear the app’s data or uninstall it; exported files in your Music folder can be deleted by you at any time. For data held by Unity, please contact Unity as described in its privacy policy.

8. Children’s privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will help.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, India, or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to or opt out of certain processing such as personalized advertising. Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, most requests should be directed to Unity. You can still contact us at any time and we will do our best to help.

10. Security

Because your audio is processed locally, it is not transmitted over the internet by the app. Ad requests are sent over encrypted connections, and the app does not allow unencrypted (HTTP) traffic.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when we add features or change ad partners. The latest version will always be available at this page, and the effective date above will be updated.
