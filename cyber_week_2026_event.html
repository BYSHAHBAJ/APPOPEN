What is Cyber VIP?

The supplied event information says players activate Cyber VIP by purchasing the Cyber VIP Card + Emote pack. During Cyber Week, eligible VIPs may receive free draws, discounts and extra benefits in selected events.

  • Cyber VIP status and its perks are limited to Cyber Week 2026.
  • Benefits vary by event; a perk may no longer be claimable after its event ends.
  • Some events might not be available at the start of Cyber Week, so later event updates may matter.
  • Vouchers have their own expiry timing and should be used before the related event finishes.
