How to follow BGMI and PUBG MOBILE events

Event dates and reward availability can differ by region and may change after an update. Treat dates shared in community posts as tentative unless they are confirmed in the in-game event centre or by an official publisher announcement.

Before claiming an event reward

  • Open the event page inside your installed game and check its start and end time.
  • Read the mission requirements carefully; some rewards require daily logins, missions or an invitation action.
  • Check the reward preview and claim deadline before spending UC or another in-game currency.
  • Avoid third-party pages that ask for your game password, verification code or account recovery details.

What this page is for

This page collects event-related information for quick reference. It is not a live game feed, and listings may become outdated. Always use the in-game event centre as the final source for eligibility, timing and rewards. If you spot an error on this page, please contact us.
