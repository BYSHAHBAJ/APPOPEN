What Is the Miravix X-Suit Spin in PUBG MOBILE & BGMI?

The Miravix X-Suit Spin features a themed collection built around three named X-Suit forms: Cascade Miravix, Aether Miravix and Prism Miravix. Alongside the X-Suits, the event features a Miravix - M249, themed throwable items, helmet and backpack cosmetics, profile items, vouchers and a token-based redemption system.

The main attraction is the choice between the three Miravix forms. The Miravix X-Suit Selection Crate allows the player to select one of the three forms after obtaining the crate through the Miravix X-Suit Prize Pool. Each form has a seven-star progression, from 1-Star to 7-Star. Reaching 6-Star on a form unlocks that form’s special Ace Eliminator Broadcast, which must be equipped in the Lab to be used.

The event also includes Energy progress. A draw from any prize pool in the event gives 1–3 Energy. Once the progress bar reaches 300 Energy, the listed reward is 1,000 Miravix Tokens. Duplicate X-Suit forms and repeated rewards can be converted into Miravix Tokens, and a surprise 10x token bonus may occur during duplicate conversion.

Cascade Miravix X-Suit in PUBG MOBILE & BGMI

Cascade Miravix is one of the three selectable Miravix X-Suit forms. Its progression includes named versions from 1-Star through 7-Star. The standout milestone specifically associated with Cascade is the Cascade Miravix Ace Eliminator Broadcast, unlocked by upgrading Cascade Miravix to 6-Star.

After unlocking it, equip the broadcast in the Lab. The described effect plays when the player becomes or eliminates the Ace Eliminator, with the presentation based on the current elimination count. This is a special broadcast feature linked to the form’s progression.

Aether Miravix X-Suit in PUBG MOBILE & BGMI

For the exact appearance, colours, animations and visual differences between the forms, use the in-game preview when available rather than relying on the name alone.

Prism Miravix X-Suit in PUBG MOBILE & BGMI

Prism Miravix is the third selectable form. Its listed progression also runs from 1-Star through 7-Star. The Prism Miravix Ace Eliminator Broadcast is unlocked by upgrading Prism Miravix to 6-Star and then equipping the broadcast in the Lab.

The three forms are distinct named choices within the same Miravix collection. The Selection Crate is described as letting players choose one of the three forms; it should not be interpreted as a guarantee that one crate automatically grants all three.

Miravix Energy System: 1–3 Energy per Draw

The event’s Energy system works separately from Miravix Tokens. A draw from any prize pool in the event gives 1–3 Energy. When the Energy bar reaches 300 Energy, the listed milestone reward is 1,000 Miravix Tokens.

Keep the two values separate:

  • Energy is progress toward the 300-Energy milestone.
  • Miravix Tokens are the event currency used in Reward Redemption.
  • The 300-Energy milestone grants 1,000 Miravix Tokens.

The number of draws needed to reach the milestone can vary because each draw gives 1–3 Energy. Check the progress bar after each draw to see the current total and remaining Energy.
