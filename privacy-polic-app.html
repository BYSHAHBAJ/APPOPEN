1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information the APNA FPS BOOSTER app ("the App", "we", "us", "our") handles, why, and the choices you have. By installing or using the App you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the App.

The App is a gaming utility for Android. It provides game-related tools, downloadable content, a news and leaks feed, and optional premium (VIP) features. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, phone number, email address, or password inside the App.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • VIP access key: if you buy or receive a VIP key and enter it, the key is checked online against our server. A verified key is stored encrypted on your device (Android Keystore-backed storage) so you do not need to type it again.
  • Search text: text typed in the in-app search box is used only on your device to filter posts and is not sent to us.

2.3 Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your contacts, SMS, call logs, camera or microphone data, or precise GPS location.
  • We do not read your game account, game login, or game chat, and we do not collect your in-game username or password.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2.4 On-device data (not sent to us)

Settings (such as selected game, layout choice), a cached copy of the news/leaks feed, and cached pictures are stored only on your device. Cached pictures and feed data can be removed at any time from Android Settings → Apps → APNA FPS BOOSTER → Storage → Clear cache / Clear data.

  • Analytics and ad data are kept by the respective providers according to their own retention periods. Server-side VIP/device records are kept only as long as needed for the VIP service, abuse prevention and legal compliance.
  • No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.
