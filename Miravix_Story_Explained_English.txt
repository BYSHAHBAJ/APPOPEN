INTRODUCTION: WHAT IS MIRAVIX?
============================================================

Imagine an X-Suit collection that is more than just a single outfit. It includes three different forms, star upgrades, special animations, exclusive cosmetics, and a dedicated weapon collection. The localization dump identifies this collection as the “Miravix X-Suit.”

The collection includes three forms:

1. Cascade Miravix
2. Aether Miravix
3. Prism Miravix

The dump refers to all three as forms of the Miravix X-Suit. Therefore, based on the wording in the file, it is more accurate to understand them as three selectable forms within one Miravix X-Suit collection rather than as three unrelated outfits. The text also refers to obtaining the Miravix X-Suit through the Collection System.

One important clarification: the localization text confirms the names of these forms and describes some collection mechanics, but it does not explain their complete character lore, powers, gameplay statistics, or exact visual designs. This article covers what the dump actually supports.

THE THREE FORMS AND THEIR STAR-UPGRADE SYSTEM
============================================================

One of the central features of the Miravix collection is its star progression. The dump contains entries for Cascade Miravix, Aether Miravix, and Prism Miravix at every star level from 1-Star through 7-Star.

The listed progression is:

• Cascade Miravix: 1-Star, 2-Star, 3-Star, 4-Star, 5-Star, 6-Star, and 7-Star.
• Aether Miravix: 1-Star, 2-Star, 3-Star, 4-Star, 5-Star, 6-Star, and 7-Star.
• Prism Miravix: 1-Star, 2-Star, 3-Star, 4-Star, 5-Star, 6-Star, and 7-Star.

A mission-style line also says: “Obtain any 1 Miravix X-Suit (1-Star).” This indicates that obtaining at least one 1-Star Miravix X-Suit was part of an event or progression objective.

The dump also includes a special unlock condition: “Unlocked after all 3 Miravix X-Suits are upgraded to 7-Star.” This clearly links an additional reward or feature to upgrading all three forms to 7-Star. However, that line does not name the reward, so it would not be accurate to assign it to a specific item or feature.

The file also lists Miravix-exclusive upgrade material. Its description says that it is used to upgrade an X-Suit's star level and is specifically exclusive to Miravix X-Suit star upgrades. The names “Starforge Stone (Miravix-Exclusive)” and “Star Fragment (Miravix-Exclusive)” also appear in the dump.

THE 6-STAR ACE ELIMINATOR BROADCASTS
============================================================

The Miravix star progression also has a special reward connection at 6-Star.

• Upgrading Cascade Miravix to 6-Star unlocks the Cascade Miravix Ace Eliminator Broadcast.
• Upgrading Aether Miravix to 6-Star unlocks the Aether Miravix Ace Eliminator Broadcast.
• Upgrading Prism Miravix to 6-Star unlocks the Prism Miravix Ace Eliminator Broadcast.

The text says these broadcasts can be equipped in the Lab. Their description explains that a special Ace Eliminator broadcast plays according to the current elimination count when the player becomes an Ace Eliminator or eliminates an Ace Eliminator.

This means that each form has its own named broadcast reward listed for the 6-Star level. The localization does more than mention the reward names: it also provides the unlock condition and describes how the broadcasts are used.

THE EVENT DRAW SYSTEM: ENERGY AND MIRAVIX TOKENS
============================================================

The localization text also describes an event draw system. According to the dump, drawing from any prize pool in the event can award between 1 and 3 Energy. As Energy is collected, the progress bar fills up.

When the total reaches 300 Energy, the Energy progress reward is listed as 1,000 Miravix Tokens.

These Miravix Tokens can be used to redeem items in the event's Reward Redemption section. In other words, the draw system is described as offering a chance to receive prize-pool rewards while also building Energy progress toward a token reward.

Keep one detail in mind: the text does not give a fixed Energy amount for every draw. It only says that a draw can grant 1–3 Energy. Therefore, it would be inaccurate to claim that every draw always gives 3 Energy.

THE MIRAVIX X-SUIT SELECTION CRATE
============================================================

The Miravix X-Suit Prize Pool includes a “Miravix X-Suit Selection Crate.” After obtaining this crate, the player is given the option to choose one of the three Miravix X-Suit forms:

1. Cascade Miravix
2. Aether Miravix
3. Prism Miravix

Another description explains how the crate works: the player can open it and select a Miravix X-Suit. However, if the player already owns all three Miravix X-Suit forms, the crate's reward will be dismantled into items instead.

A duplicate-conversion system is also listed. If the player receives a form they already have, it can be converted into a specified amount of Miravix Tokens. A separate line also says that once the player has obtained all three forms, subsequent duplicates are automatically dismantled into specified items.

From a collection perspective, the main purpose of the crate is to let a player choose the missing form. Once all three forms are owned, repeated crates or duplicate forms are handled through the separate conversion or dismantling system.

DUPLICATE REWARDS AND THE 10X TOKEN BONUS
============================================================

The Miravix event rules describe conversion for repeated rewards, not just duplicate X-Suit forms.

If other rewards are obtained again through a draw, they may also be converted into a certain amount of Miravix Tokens. The text additionally mentions a chance of receiving a surprise bonus during conversion, which can provide 10 times the token amount.

The 10x bonus is described as a possible surprise bonus. The dump does not say that it is guaranteed.

Miravix Tokens are intended for use in the event's Reward Redemption section. A warning also says that Miravix Tokens will be dismantled through mail after they expire. Players are therefore advised to redeem items in Reward Redemption before the expiry period.
