About APNA FPS BOOSTER

APNA FPS BOOSTER is an independent Android app and website that helps BGMI and PUBG MOBILE players understand FPS options, graphics settings and lag.

What we do

We make a free Android app that shows FPS and graphics options for supported BGMI and PUBG MOBILE versions, and we publish plain-language guides on frame rates, refresh rates and lag. More than 36,000 players use the app.

Who runs it

The app and website are made and maintained by WILDSOUL FURY, who also shares BGMI and PUBG MOBILE videos on YouTube, Instagram and Telegram. You can reach us at shahbajbusiness@gmail.com.

How we write our guides

Guides are based on how the app works and on public information about the games. We say clearly what depends on your phone or game version, avoid promising results, and review guides after major game updates. Each guide shows its last-updated date.
