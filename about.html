About Apna FPS Booster

Apna FPS Booster is an independent informational website for mobile gaming enthusiasts. We publish explainers and updates about BGMI and PUBG MOBILE, game-related events, cosmetic items, FPS and graphics settings, screenshots, video guides, and information about our FPS Booster app.

What we publish

  • Gaming update articles that explain announced or reported features and clearly distinguish speculation from confirmed information where possible.
  • Guides about FPS options, graphics settings, compatibility and device-dependent performance.
  • Original page descriptions, screenshots and video references to help visitors understand the topic before they choose to visit an external resource.
