BGMI and PUBG MOBILE lag fix guide

Quick answer: To reduce BGMI or PUBG MOBILE lag, first work out whether it is network lag (high ping, rubber-banding) or FPS stutter (uneven motion). Fix network lag with a stable connection; fix stutter by closing background apps, cooling the phone and choosing a frame rate it can hold. You only see frames up to your display's refresh rate, and no app can guarantee 90, 120 or 165 FPS on every phone.

How to fix BGMI and PUBG MOBILE lag: 7 steps

  1. Identify the cause. Delayed actions and rubber-banding point to network lag; choppy motion points to low or unstable FPS.
  2. Update and restart. Install the latest game version from its official source, then restart the phone.
  3. Free memory and storage. Close background apps and keep several GB of storage free.
  4. Use a stable connection. Prefer a strong Wi-Fi or mobile signal and pause downloads while playing.
  5. Pick a frame rate your phone can hold. A steady 60 FPS feels better than a 120 FPS setting that keeps dropping.
  6. Keep the phone cool. Remove thick cases, avoid charging during long sessions and lower graphics if it overheats.
  7. Then review UNLOCK FPS. Open APNA FPS BOOSTER features and check the options shown for your device and game version. For a full walkthrough, read the 120 FPS unlock guide.

Frequently asked questions

What is APNA FPS BOOSTER?

An independent Android app that lists FPS and graphics options for BGMI and PUBG MOBILE, with lag-fix guides. It is not made by the game publishers.

Is it an official BGMI or PUBG MOBILE app?

No. It is a third-party app. Game publishers set their own terms and may limit changes to game settings, so read our disclaimer before you decide.

Does it guarantee 120 or 165 FPS?

No. It shows options where your device, display and game version support them. Actual FPS depends on your hardware, temperature and the game.

Does a 120Hz display mean 120 FPS in the game?

No. The screen's refresh rate and the game's rendering rate are separate. The game's own graphics settings show what is available.

Why is 90 or 120 FPS missing in my game?

Your device, region or game version may not offer it. Check the options after each game update and pick the highest rate your phone can hold steadily.

Do I need root?

Not necessarily. The app can use Shizuku for supported operations. What works depends on your Android version and the feature.

Will it work on a 2GB RAM phone?

It may run, but it cannot add RAM. On low-memory phones use low graphics, a steady 60 FPS and close background apps.

Can it fix all lag?

No. Network lag (high ping, rubber-banding) is different from FPS stutter. Test both separately using the lag fix guide above.

Does it work on iPhone?

No. It is an Android app. On iPhone use the options the game already offers.

Does higher FPS drain the battery?

Yes. Higher FPS uses more power and creates more heat. A steady 60 or 90 FPS often beats a 120 FPS setting that keeps dropping.

The game crashes after I change FPS. What now?

Restart the phone and choose a lower, compatible option. If it continues, restore default settings and clear the game's cache.
