BGMI and PUBG MOBILE lag fix guide

Quick answer: To reduce BGMI or PUBG MOBILE lag, first work out whether it is network lag (high ping, rubber-banding) or FPS stutter (uneven motion). Fix network lag with a stable connection; fix stutter by closing background apps, cooling the phone and choosing a frame rate it can hold. You only see frames up to your display's refresh rate, and no app can guarantee 90, 120 or 165 FPS on every phone.

How to fix BGMI and PUBG MOBILE lag: 7 steps

  1. Identify the cause. Delayed actions and rubber-banding point to network lag; choppy motion points to low or unstable FPS.
  2. Update and restart. Install the latest game version from its official source, then restart the phone.
  3. Free memory and storage. Close background apps and keep several GB of storage free.
  4. Use a stable connection. Prefer a strong Wi-Fi or mobile signal and pause downloads while playing.
  5. Pick a frame rate your phone can hold. A steady 60 FPS feels better than a 120 FPS setting that keeps dropping.
  6. Keep the phone cool. Remove thick cases, avoid charging during long sessions and lower graphics if it overheats.
  7. Then review UNLOCK FPS. Open APNA FPS BOOSTER features and check the options shown for your device and game version. For a full walkthrough, read the 120 FPS unlock guide.
