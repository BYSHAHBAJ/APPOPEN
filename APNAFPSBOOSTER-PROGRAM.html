Program details and participation requirements

Welcome to the APNA FPS BOOSTER Creator Partnership Program. If you create BGMI, PUBG MOBILE, gaming, or technology content on YouTube, you can make a promotional video for APNA FPS BOOSTER and apply for VIP Channel Access as a reward.

How to Create Your Video

1. Watch the Reference Demo Video

Watch our reference video to understand how to present the FPS unlock options and demonstrate the process.

Reference Video: https://youtu.be/X7FsFahn5uo

2. Create Your Promotional Video

You can choose either of the following methods:

  • Option A — Create Your Own Video: Record your screen, explain the available FPS unlock options, and demonstrate the process.
  • Option B — Use Our Demo Video: Use our demo footage and add your own voice-over, AI voice-over, commentary, captions, or meaningful editing.

You do not need to explain every feature of APNA FPS BOOSTER. Focus mainly on the FPS unlock options and show how the process works.

3. Test It in the Game

After demonstrating the FPS unlock process, launch the supported game and show the selected FPS option in the game's settings. If possible, demonstrate gameplay to show that the selected option is available on your device.

Do not make fake performance claims or show results you have not verified. FPS availability and performance may vary depending on the device and game version.

4. Publish Your Video

  • Upload the video to your YouTube channel.
  • Keep the video Public and accessible during the review process.
  • Include our official website link in the video description: https://apnafpsbooster.com
  • Submit your published YouTube video URL through the application form.

Application Form: https://forms.gle/x81FgQozYadUnhMB6

VIP Reward System

Regular YouTube Videos

  • Reward: 1 Month of VIP Channel Access.
  • Your video must meet the program requirements and pass verification.

YouTube Shorts

  • Standard Reward: 7 Days of VIP Channel Access.
  • If your Short achieves good views and engagement, you may qualify for 1 Month of VIP Channel Access, subject to review and approval.

Renewing Your VIP Access

After your current VIP plan expires, you may create and publish another new, eligible promotional video and submit it for review. If approved, you may receive VIP access again. Each repeat reward requires a new eligible video and is subject to verification and approval.

Important Rules

  1. Your video must promote APNA FPS BOOSTER and explain the FPS unlock options.
  2. You must demonstrate the actual process. In-game testing is strongly expected.
  3. You may use your own footage or our demo footage with your own voice-over, AI voice-over, commentary, captions, or editing.
  4. Simply re-uploading our demo without meaningful changes is not eligible.
  5. Your video must remain Public and accessible during the review process.
  6. The official website link must be included in the video description.
  7. Submit the correct YouTube video URL and accurate details.
  8. VIP access is not automatic. Every submission is subject to verification and approval.
  9. Reward eligibility may depend on video quality, compliance with the requirements, and performance where applicable.
  10. Repeat rewards require a new eligible promotional video after the previous VIP plan expires.
