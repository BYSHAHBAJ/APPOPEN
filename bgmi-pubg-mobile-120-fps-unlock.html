How to unlock 120 FPS in BGMI and PUBG MOBILE on Android

Last updated 27 October 2026. Written for APNA FPS BOOSTER 5.2.0.

Whether you can run BGMI or PUBG MOBILE at 120 FPS depends on your phone, your game version and your region. This guide shows what to check first, how to try the UNLOCK FPS feature in APNA FPS BOOSTER, how to confirm the result, and what to do if 120 FPS does not appear.

Quick answer: 120 FPS needs three things at once: a display that refreshes at 120 Hz or higher, a game version and region that offer the option, and a tool or setting that exposes it. The UNLOCK FPS feature in APNA FPS BOOSTER lists the options available for the game and version you select. It cannot raise your screen's refresh rate, and it cannot guarantee 120 FPS on every phone.

What to check before you start

  • An Android phone. APNA FPS BOOSTER does not run on iPhone. On iOS, use the frame-rate options the game already offers.
  • A display that can show 120 FPS. You only see 120 FPS if your screen refreshes at 120 Hz or higher. The frame-rate comparison table shows what each rate needs.
  • The right game and region. BGMI, BGMI AIR, PUBG MOBILE Global, PUBG MOBILE FLASH and the Taiwan, Korea and Vietnam editions are listed separately under supported games and regions. BGMI and PUBG MOBILE can need different FPS configurations.
  • An official, up-to-date game. Install the game from the official source for your region and keep it updated.
  • The app from this website. Get APNA FPS BOOSTER from the download section and review the Android permissions before you install it.
  • A backup. Keep a backup of important game configuration files before you change anything.

How to check your screen's refresh rate

Open Android Settings and search for "refresh rate", "smooth display" or "motion smoothness". The name varies by brand. If your phone only offers 60 Hz, you will not see more than 60 FPS, even if the game lists a higher option. The frame-rate demo at the top of the home page also estimates your screen's refresh rate in your browser.

How to unlock 120 FPS with APNA FPS BOOSTER

These steps follow the UNLOCK FPS feature. Screens and labels can change between app and game versions, so follow the on-screen instructions if they differ from this list.

  1. Install the app. Download APNA FPS BOOSTER from the download section and review the permissions Android shows.
  2. Open UNLOCK FPS. Start the app and go to the UNLOCK FPS feature.
  3. Select your game and version. Choose BGMI or the PUBG MOBILE region you actually play, then the matching game version.
  4. Choose a listed FPS option. Pick 120 FPS if the app offers it for your setup. If it is not listed, choose the highest option the app shows that your phone can hold steadily.
  5. Follow the on-screen instructions. Read each prompt before you confirm it.
  6. Restart the game if asked. Then check the result inside the game, as described below.

Do you need root? Not necessarily. The app can use Shizuku for supported operations without rooting your device. What works depends on your Android version and the feature you use, so follow the app's own instructions.

How to check that 120 FPS worked

Open BGMI or PUBG MOBILE, go to Settings, then Graphics, and look at the frame-rate list. The game's own settings screen is the source of truth. A video of someone else's phone does not prove that yours can do it.

Play a short training session or match and watch for stutter, heat and battery drain. A steady 90 FPS usually feels better than a 120 FPS setting that keeps dropping, so lower the option if your phone struggles.

What the in-game labels mean

The game shows frame-rate choices as labels such as High, Ultra or Extreme, and graphics styles such as Smooth. Which label matches 60, 90 or 120 FPS depends on the game version and your device. Read the list on your own screen instead of copying someone else's settings.

Frequently asked questions

Can every Android phone run BGMI or PUBG MOBILE at 120 FPS?

No. You need a display that refreshes at 120 Hz or higher, a game version and region that offer the option, and hardware that can keep up. Many phones will top out at 60 or 90 FPS.

Do I need root to unlock 120 FPS?

Not necessarily. APNA FPS BOOSTER can use Shizuku for supported operations. What works depends on your Android version and the feature you use.

Is 120 FPS always better than 90 FPS?

No. 120 FPS only feels smoother if your phone holds it steadily. If it overheats or drops frames, a steady 90 or 60 FPS is usually the better choice.

Can the 120 FPS option disappear after a game update?

Yes, it can. Updates can change which options a game offers. Check the game's graphics settings after every update and use the latest app release from this website.

Does APNA FPS BOOSTER work on iPhone?

No. It is an Android app. On iPhone, use the frame-rate and graphics options the game already offers.
